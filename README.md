
# 🎮 Endless Tic-Tac-Toe

A modern, strategic twist on the classic Tic-Tac-Toe game — built natively for **Android using Kotlin & Jetpack Compose**.

Unlike traditional Tic-Tac-Toe, **Endless Tic-Tac-Toe** keeps the board constantly evolving. Each player can have a maximum of **3 pieces** on the board. When a player places their 4th piece, their **oldest piece disappears**, creating an endless battle of strategy, prediction, and timing. ♾️

## ✨ Features

* ♟️ **Endless Gameplay** — Your oldest piece disappears when you place your 4th piece.
* 👥 **2–4 Player Support** — Play with 2, 3, or 4 players.
* 🤖 **Single Player Mode** — Challenge the built-in AI with adjustable difficulty.
* 👫 **Local Multiplayer** — Play pass-and-play with friends on the same device.
* 🌐 **Online Multiplayer** — Create or join real-time online rooms.
* 🏠 **Custom Game Rooms** — Host rooms and manage players through an online lobby.
* ⚡ **Real-Time WebSocket Networking** — Live game-state synchronization with a custom WebSocket relay system.
* 🔄 **Connection Handling** — Designed to handle player disconnections and synchronize game states.
* 😄 **Live Emoji Reactions** — React to events during online matches.
* 🎨 **Modern Jetpack Compose UI** — Fully built with Compose and Material Design.
* ✨ **Smooth Animations** — Animated piece placement and dynamic game interactions.
* 📱 **Responsive Layout** — Optimized for different Android screen sizes.
* 🔊 **Custom Sound Effects** — Audio feedback for a more immersive experience.

## 🎯 Game Concept

The objective is simple: **get three of your pieces in a winning pattern before your opponents do.**

But there's a catch...

You can only keep **three pieces** on the board at any time.

When you place your **4th piece**, your oldest piece is removed.

This creates a constantly changing battlefield where previously safe positions can suddenly become vulnerable.

> **Every move changes the future. ♾️**

## 🌐 Multiplayer Architecture

The online mode uses a custom **WebSocket-based relay architecture** to provide real-time communication between players.

The networking system handles:

* Room creation and joining
* Player management
* Lobby synchronization
* Turn synchronization
* Real-time board updates
* Player disconnections
* Game-state synchronization
* Online emoji reactions
* 2, 3, and 4-player matches
